Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This page is a starting point, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified attorney before publishing your site.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Orynza Global LLC ("Orynza," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use this website or purchase our Services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact form data: name, email address, selected service, and project details you submit.
  • Payment data: we do not collect or store payment card details. Checkout is processed entirely by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record — see their privacy policy for how they handle payment data.
  • Basic technical data: standard web server logs (e.g. IP address, browser type) collected automatically by our hosting provider.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

  • Paddle.com — payment processing and checkout (Merchant of Record)
  • Formspree — delivers contact form submissions to us by email
  • Our hosting provider — serves the website files
  • Google Fonts — loads web fonts used on this site

Each of these providers may process limited technical data (such as IP address) as part of delivering their service to us.

4. How We Use Information

We use contact form submissions to respond to inquiries and deliver quotes. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and order-related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our Services and comply with legal obligations, such as tax recordkeeping.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information (for example, under GDPR if you are in the EU/UK, or CCPA if you are a California resident). To exercise these rights, contact us via our Contact page.

7. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of New Mexico, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

8. Contact

Privacy-related questions or requests can be sent via our Contact page.
