FAQ

CV design questions

What's "ATS-friendly" mean?

Applicant Tracking Systems scan CVs before a human sees them. We use formatting that parses cleanly — avoiding graphics-heavy layouts, tables, or columns that can confuse these systems.

Do you write the content, or just design it?

This package focuses on structuring and formatting the content you provide. If you need help writing or rewriting content, mention that in your brief and we'll scope it together.

What file formats do I get?

A final PDF plus an editable source file, so you can make small updates yourself later.
